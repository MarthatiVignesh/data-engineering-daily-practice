DAY 2 - DATA ENGINEERING CASE STUDY
====================================

CASE STUDY: ONLINE SHOPPING COMPANY
------------------------------------

BUSINESS SCENARIO
-----------------

An online shopping company wants to store and analyze
large amounts of business data.

The company has the following data:

1. Customer information
2. Product information
3. Order information
4. Sales transactions
5. Store information
6. Date information
7. Website logs
8. Customer reviews
9. Clickstream data
10. Product images


BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS
---------------------

Management wants to analyze:

- Total sales
- Sales by product
- Sales by customer
- Sales by city
- Monthly sales
- Product performance


DATA STORAGE DECISION
---------------------

For structured and curated business data used for reporting
and analytics, we choose a DATA WAREHOUSE.

Reason:

- Sales data is structured.
- Customer data is structured.
- Product data is structured.
- Management requires analytical reports.
- Historical data needs to be analyzed.
- Business intelligence and dashboards require curated data.


For raw and diverse data such as:

- Website logs
- JSON events
- Customer reviews
- Clickstream data
- Images

we choose a DATA LAKE.

Reason:

- Data comes in different formats.
- Raw data can be preserved.
- Structured, semi-structured and unstructured data can be stored.
- The data may be used later for analytics or machine learning.

---------------------

DATA MODELLING DECISION
-----------------------

For the analytical Data Warehouse, we choose a STAR SCHEMA.

Reason:

- There is a central sales fact table.
- Sales contains measurable values.
- Customer, Product, Store and Date are dimensions.
- The structure is simple.
- It requires fewer joins.
- It is suitable for reporting and analytical queries.

GRAIN
-----

The grain of the Sales Fact table is:

"One row represents one product sold in one order."


Example:

Order_ID    Product     Quantity    Sales
------------------------------------------
1001        Laptop         1        50000
1001        Mouse          2         2000
1001        Keyboard       1         1500


Therefore, the three rows represent three product lines
within the same order.


WHY GRAIN IS IMPORTANT
----------------------

The grain tells us exactly what one row represents.

It helps us:

- Avoid duplicate counting.
- Perform correct aggregations.
- Design the fact table correctly.
- Maintain consistent calculations.


FINAL DECISION
--------------

Data Warehouse:
Use for structured and curated business data that is
required for reporting and analytics.

Data Lake:
Use for raw data in different formats such as logs,
JSON, reviews, clickstream data and images.

Star Schema:
Use for the analytical Data Warehouse because it provides
a simple structure with a central fact table and dimension
tables.

Snowflake Schema:
Can be considered when dimensions contain complex
hierarchies and normalization is beneficial.

3NF:
More appropriate for highly normalized transactional or
operational database design.
