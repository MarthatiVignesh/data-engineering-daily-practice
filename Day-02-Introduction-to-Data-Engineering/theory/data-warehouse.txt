DAY 2 - INTRODUCTION TO DATA ENGINEERING
========================================

TOPIC: DATA WAREHOUSE FUNDAMENTALS

1. WHAT IS A DATA WAREHOUSE?

A Data Warehouse is a centralized system used to store and analyze
large amounts of structured data collected from different sources.

It is mainly designed for reporting, analytics, and decision-making.

Example:

A retail company may collect data from:

- Sales systems
- Customer systems
- Payment systems
- Inventory systems

This data can be brought together into a Data Warehouse so that
business users can analyze sales, customers, revenue, and other
business information.

2. PURPOSE OF A DATA WAREHOUSE

The main purpose of a Data Warehouse is to provide a central place
for analytical data.

It helps organizations:

- Analyze historical data
- Generate reports
- Identify business trends
- Support decision-making
- Combine data from multiple sources
- Perform analytical queries

3. CHARACTERISTICS OF A DATA WAREHOUSE

Important characteristics include:

A. Subject-oriented
-------------------
Data is organized around important business subjects such as:

- Customers
- Sales
- Products
- Employees

B. Integrated
-------------
Data can come from multiple sources and is brought together into
a consistent format.

C. Time-variant
---------------
A Data Warehouse stores historical data, allowing organizations
to analyze how information changes over time.

D. Non-volatile
---------------
Data stored in a Data Warehouse is mainly used for analysis.
It is not continuously changed like data in an operational system.

4. SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Suppose an online shopping company has:

Customers
Orders
Products
Payments

The operational systems generate data every day.

The data can be collected and transformed before being loaded into
a Data Warehouse.

Then analysts can answer questions such as:

- What were the total sales this year?
- Which product generated the highest revenue?
- Which month had the highest sales?
- Which customers purchased the most?

6. KEY IDEA

A Data Warehouse is mainly designed for analytical workloads.

It stores integrated and historical data so that organizations can
perform reporting, analysis, and business decision-making.
