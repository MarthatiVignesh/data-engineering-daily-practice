DAY 2 - INTRODUCTION TO DATA ENGINEERING
========================================

TOPIC: DATA MART

1. WHAT IS A DATA MART?

A Data Mart is a smaller, focused collection of data designed
for a particular business department, subject, or group of users.

Examples:

- Sales Data Mart
- Finance Data Mart
- HR Data Mart
- Marketing Data Mart

A Data Mart usually contains a subset of the data needed by a
specific department.

2. PURPOSE OF A DATA MART

The main purpose of a Data Mart is to provide relevant data to
a specific business area.

It helps users:

- Access relevant data quickly
- Perform department-specific analysis
- Generate reports
- Support business decisions
- Reduce the complexity of accessing enterprise data

3. DATA MART EXAMPLE

Suppose a company has a Data Warehouse containing:

- Customer data
- Sales data
- Product data
- Employee data
- Finance data

The company can create separate Data Marts:

              DATA WAREHOUSE
                    |
          +---------+---------+
          |         |         |
          v         v         v
       Sales      Finance     HR
       Mart        Mart       Mart

The Sales team can mainly work with the Sales Data Mart instead
of working with the complete enterprise Data Warehouse.

4. RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN DATA MART AND DATA WAREHOUSE

A Data Warehouse is generally enterprise-wide and can contain
data for many business areas.

A Data Mart is smaller and focused on a particular business
department or subject.

Example:

Enterprise Data Warehouse
          |
          +---- Sales Data Mart
          |
          +---- Finance Data Mart
          |
          +---- HR Data Mart
          |
          +---- Marketing Data Mart

5. TYPES OF DATA MARTS

A. Dependent Data Mart
----------------------
A dependent Data Mart gets its data from an existing Data Warehouse.

Data Warehouse
      |
      v
Data Mart

B. Independent Data Mart
------------------------
An independent Data Mart obtains data directly from operational
systems or other sources without depending on a central Data
Warehouse.

Source Systems
      |
      v
Independent Data Mart

C. Hybrid Data Mart
--------------------
A hybrid Data Mart can combine data from a Data Warehouse with
data from other sources.

6. DATA WAREHOUSE VS DATA MART

Data Warehouse:
- Enterprise-wide
- Contains data for multiple business areas
- Larger in scope
- Supports organization-wide analytics

Data Mart:
- Department or subject-specific
- Smaller in scope
- Contains relevant data for a particular group
- Supports focused analytics

7. KEY IDEA

A Data Mart is a smaller and focused analytical data store
designed for a particular department, business function, or
subject area.

A Data Warehouse can serve the entire organization, while
Data Marts provide focused access to specific areas of the
business.
